Research Engineer Intern, [Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy](https://www.mpi-sp.org/) (Apr 2026 – Present).
Post-quantum cryptographic engineering research with [Peter Schwabe](https://cryptojedi.org/peter/index.shtml) and [Vadim Lyubashevsky](https://research.ibm.com/people/vadim-lyubashevsky).

- Conducting post-quantum cryptographic engineering research, designing and analyzing a novel noise distribution for ML-KEM at elevated security levels.
- Evaluating cryptographic hardness assumptions and failure probability bounds through large-scale probabilistic simulations, informing security parameter selection for real-world deployments ([GitHub](https://github.com/cryptojedi/kaiburr)).
- Implemented formally-verifiable, constant-time AVX2 assembly in Jasmin for the modified scheme, achieving 3–5× speedup over FrodoKEM at over 10× the security margin, benchmarked across multiple x86-64 platforms.
- Porting the implementation to hand-written assembly for OpenTitan's cryptographic accelerator (OTBN), extending evaluation to embedded secure-element hardware.
