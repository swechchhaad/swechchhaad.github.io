- Developed the *tolerant*, a novel invariant that extends and strengthens classical discriminant theory.
- Proved a closed-form formula for the tolerant via discriminants and resultants, resolving an open question in prior work.
- Established and proved structural and algebraic properties of the tolerant; presented findings at multiple research conferences.
